Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

TL;DR — Saved tabs stay in your browser — no account, no remote analytics, no cross-site tracking. To group and search intelligently, Neat Freak's AI features (on by default) send tab titles, URLs, and short page summaries to a confidential AI backend that processes them inside a hardware secure enclave: the content isn't stored, isn't used to train models, and can't be read by us or the AI provider. No API key needed. Turn AI off in Settings to keep everything fully on-device.

What Neat Freak stores on your device

Saved sessions and settings live in chrome.storage.local. AI processing is described below:

  • Saved tab sessions — for each session: the URLs, titles, favicon URLs, and a short text summary of each page at the time you saved it.
  • Tab grouping metadata — folder names, signals, and confidence scores.
  • Your settings — capture defaults, whether AI is enabled, and a random, anonymous install identifier.

All of this lives in your browser's extension storage. Uninstalling Neat Freak removes it.

Prepared suggestions and Keep choices are cached in browser-session storage until the browser session ends. A local daily counter limits automatic AI attempts; it contains no tab content and is not analytics.

Local diagnostics: enabled by default for troubleshooting. Records event times, suggestion counts, review/Keep actions, close/restore results, durations, error categories and extension source locations. Random per-tidy references connect related events. No titles, URLs, page content, form values, credentials or install identifiers are recorded. Limited to 500 events and seven days, cleaned on extension activity. Disable, clear or export it in Settings. Nothing is uploaded automatically; you choose whether to share an export.

What gets sent off your device (AI features — on by default)

When AI is enabled (the default), tidying, requesting suggestions, or running a smart search transmits, for the relevant tabs: their titles, URLs, domains, and optional short page summaries (and, for search, your query). This goes to a confidential AI backend operated for Neat Freak, with two key properties:

  • A relay service that hands out short-lived usage keys (to rate-limit abuse) receives only an anonymous, random install identifier — never your tab content, and not tied to your identity.
  • The AI model runs inside a hardware secure enclave (confidential computing, provided by Tinfoil). Your tab content is processed there and cannot be read by Neat Freak's operators or by the AI provider, is not retained, and is not used to train models.

No OpenAI key — or any key — is required from you.

Turn it off any time: disable "Use AI…" in Settings and grouping runs entirely in your browser — no tab data leaves your device.

Background suggestions: background preparation starts off. Enabling "Prepare tab suggestions in the background," along with nudges, lets Neat Freak inspect inactive candidate tabs and, with AI on, send titles, URLs and optional summaries before you click Save. Suggestions cover regular windows; incognito tabs are excluded. Tabs close only after a Save & close action.

About page summaries: candidate pages may contain sensitive text, such as emails or documents. Summaries may be extracted while preparing suggestions or tidying, including from tabs you ultimately keep. Turning off summaries still allows titles and URLs to be sent when AI is on; turning off AI keeps processing local. Form checks run locally before closing and return no field values. Detected editable regions are excluded from summaries. Detection is best-effort, especially for custom editors.

What we do NOT collect

  • No remotely collected analytics or telemetry. Local diagnostics stay on your device unless you export and share them.
  • No user accounts. There is no Neat Freak account system.
  • No advertising identifiers. We do not show ads.
  • No tracking across sessions or devices. Each install is independent.
  • No selling or renting of your data. We transfer data only to the confidential AI backend described above, solely to provide the AI features you've enabled.

Permissions and why we need them

  • storage — to save your sessions and settings on your device.
  • tabs — to read open-tab metadata for counts, suggestions and tidying, close tabs after a user action, and reopen URLs on Restore.
  • scripting — to extract optional summaries from candidate pages when preparing suggestions or tidying, check locally for unsaved input, and display the floating panel.
  • notifications — to let you know when a save finishes, so you can move on.
  • alarms — to check your clutter threshold and prepare suggestions when you have enabled background preparation.
  • Host access (http/https) — to inspect candidate pages for the features described above, display the panel, and reach the AI backend when AI is enabled.

Data retention

Sessions stay in your browser until you delete them, uninstall, or the configured retention limit removes older completed sessions. A storage-size backstop can also remove older completed sessions. Pending sessions are protected. There is no remote backup or sync.

Children's privacy

Neat Freak does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. Since we don't collect data from anyone, this is somewhat redundant — but we state it explicitly.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the "Last updated" date above and publish the new version at the same URL. Material changes (e.g., adding any kind of server-side data handling) will be announced in the extension's update notes.
