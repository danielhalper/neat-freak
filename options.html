Settings

Configure capture defaults and AI features.

Capture Defaults

When nudges are enabled and you're above your tab threshold, checks inactive tabs across windows before you ask. With AI on, titles, URLs and optional page summaries are sent for processing. Automatic AI checks have a daily limit; you can still Refresh manually. Tabs close only when you choose Save & close.

Once your saved tabs exceed this, the oldest whole sessions are cleaned up automatically. 0 = keep everything. Sessions with tabs you haven't closed yet are never removed.

AI features Beta

When enabled, AI picks which tabs to save in Smart scope, prepares suggestions when requested or when background preparation is enabled, names folders by workstream, and powers natural-language search. Titles, URLs and optional page summaries are processed by a confidential, enclave-based model. Turn AI off to keep processing fully local. Disabling summaries alone still allows titles and URLs to be sent. Still in beta — expect rough edges.

Local diagnostics

Records suggestion counts, review and Keep actions, timing, close/restore results and error categories. No page titles, URLs, page content, form values or credentials. Stored only in this browser for up to 7 days or 500 events, whichever comes first. Nothing is uploaded automatically. Save settings to change recording.

Tab habits log

Off by default. For each tab: its site (e.g. github.com), when it opened, whether a link opened it, when it was in front and for roughly how long, whether Memory Saver unloaded it, whether each suggestion offered it, and whether you closed it, saved it, restored it or chose Keep. Label open tabs records your own call on each one. No full URLs, page titles or page content. Kept only in this browser for up to 30 days or 2,000 closed tabs, never uploaded. Export it to analyse it yourself. Save settings to change recording.
